About RescueBite

RescueBite is a community-driven platform connecting food donors with those in need. We rescue surplus food from restaurants, stores, and individuals, and deliver it to homeless individuals and families through our network of volunteers and NGO partners.

🤝 Our Mission

To eliminate food waste while addressing food insecurity by creating a seamless connection between surplus food and those who need it.

🌍 Our Vision

A world where no one goes hungry and no edible food goes to waste. Every meal matters, every person counts.

💚 Our Values

Compassion, sustainability, community collaboration, and dignity for all. We believe in treating everyone with respect.

Our Impact

Together with our volunteers, donors, and NGO partners, we're making real change happen every day.
